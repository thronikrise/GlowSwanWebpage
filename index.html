Common Questions

How long does one bar last?
With proper drainage between uses, each 100g bar typically lasts 4–6 weeks, depending on usage.
Is it safe for sensitive skin?
Absolutely. Our cold-process soaps retain natural glycerin, making them gentler than commercial soaps. The Aloe Vera | Mint variant is especially recommended for sensitive skin. However, we always recommend a patch test if you have specific allergies.
How does WhatsApp ordering work?
Simply tap any 'Order on WhatsApp' button, and it opens a pre-filled chat with us. Share your delivery address and preferred variant, and we'll confirm your order with payment details. It's that simple!
What about delivery charges?
Delivery charges vary by location and will be communicated during your WhatsApp conversation. We strive to keep them as reasonable as possible.
Are the soaps vegan and cruelty-free?
Yes! All GlowSwan soaps are 100% cruelty-free. We never test on animals, and our formulations use only plant-based ingredients and essential oils.
What makes cold-process soap different?
Unlike commercial soaps that use high heat (which destroys beneficial compounds), our cold-process method preserves the natural glycerin and the full potency of botanical ingredients, resulting in a richer, more nourishing bar.
